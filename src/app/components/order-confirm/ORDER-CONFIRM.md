# OrderConfirmComponent

## Mo ta
Trang xac nhan dat hang thanh cong. Hien thi ma don hang va thong tin lien he.

## Route
`/confirm/:orderId` (lazy loaded)

## Chuc nang
- Icon thanh cong (checkmark xanh)
- Hien thi ma don hang
- Thong tin lien he cua hang (SDT, dia chi)
- Nut "Tiep tuc mua sam" quay ve trang chu

## Dependencies
- `ActivatedRoute` - Lay orderId tu URL
- `Router` - Dieu huong ve trang chu
