# CartPanelComponent

## Mo ta
Panel truot tu ben phai hien thi gio hang. Co backdrop de dong.

## Selector
`app-cart-panel`

## Chuc nang
- Slide-in tu ben phai voi animation CSS
- Backdrop mo de dong khi click ra ngoai
- Hien thi danh sach san pham trong gio:
  - Hinh anh, ten, gia
  - Nut +/- thay doi so luong
  - Nut xoa san pham
- Tong tien va so luong
- Nut "Dat hang" chuyen den /checkout
- Trang thai rong khi chua co san pham

## Dependencies
- `CartService` - Quan ly gio hang va trang thai panel
- `Router` - Dieu huong den checkout
